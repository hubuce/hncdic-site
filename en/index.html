ABOUT CHANGDING

ABOUT US

Insight Across Cycles
Execution Across Transactions

Hainan Changding Investment Co., Ltd. is a professional investment and investment banking advisory firm based in Hainan Free Trade Port and serving global markets. We focus on corporate finance, mergers and acquisitions, capital strategy, cross-border investment and financing, industrial investment, and capital markets research, serving enterprises, project sponsors, and industry partners throughout the transaction lifecycle.

We begin with industrial fundamentals and intrinsic value. After identifying material risks, we design practical transaction structures and coordinate capital, industry, market, and professional resources. Changding values long-term partnerships and helps clients build sustainable capital capabilities beyond the outcome of any single financing.

ExpertiseResearch and judgment as the foundation of every transaction
